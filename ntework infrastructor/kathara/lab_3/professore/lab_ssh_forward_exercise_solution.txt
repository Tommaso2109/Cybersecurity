Solution point 2)

(uncomment line 11 of r1.startup and r2.startup, required by the exercise)
on pc2: ssh -v -NR 5000:localhost:22 s1user@10.0.1.1
on pc1: ssh -v -NL 6000:localhost:5000 s1user@10.0.0.1
on pc1: ssh pc2user@localhost -p 6000

In this way, the traffic exchanged between the nodes always uses port 22.
